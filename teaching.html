Course materials from my teaching at Vilnius University.

Economic Theory II

Vilnius University, Faculty of Economics and Business · Teacher (Coordinator: Dr. Eglė Jakučionytė)

An intermediate macroeconomic theory course. Lectures move from the Solow growth model and the overlapping generations model, through dynamic consumption saving and IS LM, to a full production economy with firms and households, fiscal policy and Ricardian equivalence, and the role of money.

The slides are based on Garin, Lester, and Sims, Intermediate Macroeconomics, a free textbook.
