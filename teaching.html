Teaching

Course materials from my teaching as a lecturer at Vilnius University.

Economic Theory II

Vilnius University, Faculty of Economics and Business · Second year bachelor's course · Spring semesters 2023, 2025 and 2026

An intermediate macroeconomic theory course. My part covers the first six weeks. The lectures build an economy piece by piece: the household's spending over time, then its work, then firms. Households and firms then meet in markets, and the course ends with the long run.

The slides are based on Garin, Lester, and Sims, Intermediate Macroeconomics, a free textbook.
